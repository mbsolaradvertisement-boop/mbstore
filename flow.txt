Project Vision

MB Store will be a multi-company B2B marketplace for industrial, electrical, solar, automation, or any other products.

Instead of:

Customer → Add to Cart → Checkout

It becomes:

Customer → Browse Products → Contact Seller → Negotiate → Order Offline

This makes the platform much simpler while still very useful.

Features that will make MB Store stand out

Rather than cloning SunStore exactly, I'd add a few differentiators:

Multi-company catalogs: One platform where many companies manage their own product catalogs under separate seller accounts.
Lead management: Every inquiry becomes a lead that sellers can track through stages like New → Contacted → Quoted → Won/Lost.
Role-based dashboards: Each role sees only what's relevant to them, with tailored analytics.
Advanced product information: Rich specifications, downloadable datasheets, certifications, videos, and comparison tools.
Company storefronts: Each company gets its own public profile with products, contact details, and branding.
Inquiry-first workflow: Replace the cart with a streamlined "Request Quote" flow that can include quantity, project details, location, and attachments.
Scalable permissions: Keep the architecture flexible so future roles (such as regional managers or company admins) can be added without major changes.
Development roadmap

I recommend building this in phases:

Phase 1: Project setup, authentication, role-based access, MySQL schema.
Phase 2: Admin dashboard, company management, categories, brands.
Phase 3: Seller dashboard and product management.
Phase 4: Customer-facing website with search, filters, product pages, and inquiry flow.
Phase 5: Support dashboard, notifications, reporting, polishing, and deployment.
